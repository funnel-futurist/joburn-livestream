## About (max 1000 chars)

A 24/7 lo-fi coworking forge for founders, operators, and anyone reaching for the next level.

Building anything real is lonely. The drive doesn't always make sense to the people around you. Nobody really sees the work — the rewriting, the doubting, the staying up.

It's not a community in the join-the-Discord sense. It's a 24/7 place. Open the tab when you start a session. Stay as long as you need. See the same blacksmith doing the work next to you. The same capybara, present. The same room, always open.

The work runs on 50/10 Pomodoro cycles. Four cycles, then a 30-minute walk. Music shifts with the phase — beats for focus, drone for the breaks.

It's a small way to say thanks. For showing up. For doing the hard thing.

## Stream Description (max ~5000 chars)

**Forge FM — a 24/7 lo-fi coworking forge for founders and operators.**

Sync your work to the cycle:

⚒️ 50 minutes focused work (the blacksmith is at the keyboard, head down)
🌿 10 minutes break (he meditates, or works on the sword — the self)
🏔️ Every 4th cycle: 30-minute walk outside (touching grass)

After the walk, the loop repeats. 24 hours a day. Always open.

**The motif: the sword is the self.**

Every hammer strike removes another impurity. Every pause is space to feel where the cracks are. The work isn't always at the keyboard — sometimes it's on the cushion, or out walking, or quietly hammering on the parts of you that still need work.

Identity is the foundation under everything you build. Crack the foundation, the whole stack cracks.

**Music strategy:**

Music shifts with the phase to support the cognitive state you're in:
• 75 BPM lo-fi beats during focus (proven optimal for sustained attention)
• 40 BPM ambient drone during meditation breaks (parasympathetic, deep rest)
• 45 BPM contemplative during hammer breaks (reflective work)
• 65 BPM walking music during the long break (embodied break)

Subtle binaural undertones run beneath the music for the cognitive state of each phase. **For maximum benefit, listen with headphones.** Speaker listeners still get the ambient music; headphone listeners get the binaural entrainment too.

**Why this exists:**

Building anything real is lonely. The drive doesn't always make sense to the people around you. Some of us needed a place where the work itself is the company. A 24/7 room where you can see someone else doing the same kind of thing.

This isn't a community in the join-the-Discord sense. No app to download, no group to be active in. Just a stream. Pull it up when you start a session. Leave when you're done.

It's a small way to say thanks. For showing up. For staying.

— Joburn
